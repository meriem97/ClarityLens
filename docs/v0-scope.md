\# ClarityLens V0



ClarityLens V0 checks one mobile screenshot locally to identify whether

the primary action may be too small or too close to adjacent actions.



\## Included



1\. Upload screenshot

2\. Select device pixel ratio

3\. Normalize to 390 CSS px

4\. Lazy-load Rust/WASM engine

5\. Measure CTA height

6\. Measure adjacent action spacing

7\. Show one annotated finding



\## Excluded



\- Screenshot-type selection

\- Page-padding measurement

\- Text or heading analysis

\- Product-card analysis

\- Colour analysis

\- Overall Clarity Score

\- Multiple findings

\- Result-card export

\- Figma-concept request

\- Lead capture

\- URL scanning

\- AI advice

\- Accounts

\- Analytics
